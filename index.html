⚙️ Mechanics Quick Notes

Key material extracted from your Mechanics notes.

Potential Energy

For a conservative force, the potential-energy difference between two points is Ub − Ua = − ∫rarb F⃗ · dr⃗. The notes emphasize that only the difference in potential energy has physical meaning; an arbitrary constant may be added to both values.

Ub − Ua = −∫ F⃗·dr⃗
For a uniform gravitational field: U(h) = mgh (with ground chosen as zero reference).

Inverse-Square Force

For an inverse-square central force, f(r) = A/r². Taking the potential at infinity as the reference gives the potential-energy expression shown in the notes.

U(r) = A/r
Examples of inverse-square forces in the notes include gravitational and electrostatic forces.

Linear Harmonic Oscillator

For a spring with equilibrium length r₀, the force is F⃗ = −k(r − r₀) r̂. Taking the potential energy at equilibrium to be zero gives the spring potential-energy form.

U(r) = ½k(r − r₀)²
The spring force is conservative.

Conservation of Mechanical Energy

Combining the work-energy theorem with the potential-energy relation gives conservation of mechanical energy.

Ka + Ua = Kb + Ub = E
For an object projected upward in constant gravity: v = √(v₀² − 2gh).

Force as Gradient of Potential Energy

For one-dimensional motion, a small displacement gives ΔU = −F(x)Δx. In the limit, the force is the negative derivative of potential energy.

F(x) = −dU/dx
If the potential energy is known, the force can be found from its derivative.

Stability

An equilibrium point x₀ satisfies dU/dx|x₀ = 0. The notes use the second derivative to classify stability.

d²U/dx²|x₀ > 0 → stable equilibrium
d²U/dx²|x₀ < 0 → unstable equilibrium
If the second derivative is zero, higher derivatives are considered; the notes describe the all-zero case as neutral stability.

Energy Diagrams

An energy diagram plots total energy E and potential energy U as functions of position. Since K = E − U and kinetic energy cannot be negative, motion is restricted to regions where U ≤ E.

K = E − U
Allowed region: U ≤ E
